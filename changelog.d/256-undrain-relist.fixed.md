- A node lifted from a drain, or just started, no longer claims a group member on a record the scheduler has replaced or is about to replace (#256, #257). The scheduler may still hold the node's last draining fact and move the group off it, and the node's view may predate such a move, so the group ran split before `--group-bound-ms`. A node now puts a nonce in its facts after it starts and after each lift, the scheduler answers it with a `relisted` record after every other output of that step, and the node claims a member placed on it only once its view holds the answer. With no scheduler, such a member waits for the group bound at most. Upgrade the scheduler module along with the agents: an older scheduler never answers, so group members on a restarted or undrained node wait for the bound.
