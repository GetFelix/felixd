- The scheduler no longer moves a placement group off a node that may still run a member, or be about to claim one (#247). A member counts as running where a node's latest fact holds it (the newer fact wins if two do), and on the node the group's record places it on while that node is silent or blocked, until it takes work again, writes a draining fact while not silent, or is forgotten. Agents now say they are draining in `sys.facts` only once no claim is under way. Before, a node with slow facts, or one blocked while a claim was in flight, kept or landed its member while the rest of the group was placed and claimed elsewhere, running the group split before `--group-bound-ms`. A member on a dead node now waits for the group bound instead of being placed again after 90 s.
