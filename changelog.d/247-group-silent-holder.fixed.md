- The scheduler no longer moves a placement group off a member held by a node it judges silent (#247). A silent node still counts as running what its last fact holds and what the group's record places on it, until its own fact drops the member, a node that is not silent reports it, or the node is forgotten. Before, a live node with slow facts kept its member while the rest of the group was placed and claimed elsewhere, running the group split before `--group-bound-ms`. A member on a dead node now waits for the group bound instead of being placed again after 90 s.
