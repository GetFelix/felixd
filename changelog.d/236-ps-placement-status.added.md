- `felixd ps` shows HELD_AT (the holder's claim epoch and the assignment offset that placed it) and ASSIGN_LAG (how far that assignment is behind the one in force) while a scheduler runs, read from the placement record and the scheduler's output (#236). ASSIGN_LAG reads `ahead` when the held assignment is past the one in force and `epoch` across scheduler epochs, and `?` marks a held claim whose placement record could not be read.
