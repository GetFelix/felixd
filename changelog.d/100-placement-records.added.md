- Each claim now records who placed it on a new stream, `wl.<id>.placed`: the scheduler's assignment (its offset and the scheduler's epoch) or first claim wins and why. `felixd ps` shows it in a PLACED column while a scheduler runs, and `felixd check` reports a placement record whose epoch names no claim. Admission and `felixd run` create the stream; a workload admitted earlier has none and shows `unknown` (#100).
