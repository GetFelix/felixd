- A node that is alive but cannot land its facts no longer keeps running a group member after the scheduler forgets it and moves the group (#251). While a scheduler is wanted, an agent none of whose facts has landed for ten `--facts-every-ms` releases its group members and claims none until a fact lands and the scheduler answers it. The scheduler no longer counts a gap in which no node's facts land, such as while no scheduler was wanted, toward forgetting a node.
